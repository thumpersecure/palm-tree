🥥 What Are Coconuts?

Each request is a coconut — falling from the palm tree of privacy onto the heads of unsuspecting trackers. The more coconuts you drop, the more confused they get. Your browsing profile goes from "identifiable human" to "chaos entity browsing from 16 countries simultaneously."

🌴 How It Works

The app fires fetch requests in no-cors mode to 100+ real websites across 8 categories. Timing uses chaos mathematics (logistic map) for human-like patterns. Headers rotate through 16 geo-locations. Decoy searches pollute your profile with random interests from beekeeping to luxury yachts.

🏝️ Modes Explained

🌴 Palm Breeze — Steady mixed traffic, the classic
🥥💥 Coconut Storm — Burst mode, 3x requests, half delays
🌿 Hidden Frond — 2.5x slower, whisper-quiet stealth
🏝️ Island Hopper — Auto geo-rotation, globe-trotting chaos
