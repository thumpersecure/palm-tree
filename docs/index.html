Pine-Tree Noise

Mobile WiFi privacy noise generator
Part of the Palm-Tree / Coconuts project

📖 What is this?

Pine-Tree generates decoy web traffic directly from your phone to confuse ad trackers, data brokers, and profiling algorithms. It visits random sites across many categories, making your browsing profile look like dozens of different people.

🔒 How it works

The app fires lightweight fetch requests (using no-cors mode) to real websites across news, tech, social, shopping, and privacy categories. Each request uses randomized headers and timing based on chaos mathematics, making the traffic pattern indistinguishable from real browsing.
